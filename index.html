About this timetable

Only the courses on the 300-level course and lecturer list are shown. Other departments' courses are left out.

GST 311, ENT 311, TMC 311 and DHS 313 are taken by all three programmes, so they show under CSC, SEN and IFT as well as All. ENT 311 is offered by the ENT department.

AUL-CSC 317 and INS 312 are one class shared by CSC, SEN and IFT. IFT lists it as INS 312 with a different title, Human-Computer Interface.

When you pick a programme, any hour where two of its courses run at once is outlined in red as a clash.

No class is scheduled on Saturday. Friday follows the draft's own Friday header, which starts at 10:00 am. Where a class lists a venue in brackets that differs from the row it sits in, this page shows the bracketed venue.
